Machine learning and AI

  • Tiny CNN walkthrough

    Follow a 6×6 image through convolution, ReLU, pooling, flatten and softmax, edit any weight, and train the network yourself.

  • Tiny LLM walkthrough

    Follow a sentence through tokens, embeddings, positions and self-attention to a prediction of the next word, and edit any weight.

  • Log autoencoder walkthrough

    Find anomalies in a real SSH auth.log: follow one line through an embedding and Conv1D layers, see what the model rebuilds and what it can't, and set the threshold yourself.

Other useful tools

Made by others, and worth exploring next.

  • Transformer Explainer

    Runs a real GPT-2 model in your browser and shows how your text flows through it: embeddings, attention heads, the MLP and next-token probabilities, with a temperature control. The same steps as the Tiny LLM walkthrough, at full size.
